Overview

A professional-grade bi-color LED panel from Aputure's renowned Amaran series. Packing 672 individual LEDs with a CRI of 95+, this panel produces accurate, natural-looking light across the full range from warm tungsten (3200K) to clean daylight (5500K) — with a single dial. Stepless 0–100% dimming gives you precise control without flicker.

Dual-power design means it runs from the included AC adapter in the studio, or from two Sony NP-F batteries (sold separately) on location. The flicker-free output makes it a reliable choice for high frame rate and slow-motion video, while the included RC1 wireless remote lets you adjust brightness and colour temperature without leaving the camera position.
