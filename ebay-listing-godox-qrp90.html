Overview

The Godox QR-P90 is a 90cm (35.4") quick-release parabolic softbox with a Bowens mount. It produces a beautifully soft, wrap-around light that's ideal for video work, interviews, portraiture, and live recording. The reflective silver interior gives punchy, high-contrast output, while the included inner and front diffusers let you dial in exactly the quality of light you need — from crispy and directional to soft and even.

Setup is genuinely fast: lift the rods from the speed ring and they click into place. Breakdown is just as quick — push the button on each rod and it folds flat. The compact speed ring keeps the whole kit portable and easy to store. Compatible with any Bowens-mount strobe or continuous light, and can also be used with Profoto or Godox-mount lights via an optional adapter ring.
