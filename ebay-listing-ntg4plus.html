Overview

The RØDE NTG4+ is the broadcast-grade version of RØDE's flagship shotgun microphone, built with one key advantage over the standard NTG4: an integrated rechargeable lithium battery. That means 150+ hours of operation from a single Micro-USB charge — no phantom power required. Use it with any camera, recorder, or interface, anywhere.

Super-cardioid polar pattern delivers tight, directional pickup that locks onto your subject and rejects unwanted sound from the sides and rear. Three on-body digital switches give you a high-frequency boost, a 75Hz high-pass filter, and a -10dB pad — all without touching the gain on your camera or recorder. Includes the professional Invision shock mount to isolate handling and environmental vibration.
