Overview

The Invision Ergonomic Monitor Arm is a sturdy, full-motion desk mount offering tilt, swivel, and height adjustment for a properly ergonomic screen position. It frees up valuable desk space and takes the weight of your monitor off the surface entirely — a worthwhile upgrade for any setup.

This unit is mechanically in excellent shape: all joints are firm, hold their position perfectly, and the full range of motion functions exactly as it should. It does show cosmetic wear from previous use — surface scratches on the arm body and a small crack in one of the plastic cable management covers. These are purely visual and have no impact on the strength or performance of the mount. Please review the photos for full detail before purchasing.
