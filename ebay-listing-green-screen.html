Overview

The GSKAIWEN Portable Chromakey Green Screen Kit is a complete backdrop and stand system designed for video production, live streaming, photography, and virtual meetings. The 2.4m × 4m green backdrop is non-translucent, non-reflective, and machine washable. It does have some creasing from storage, but these iron out easily or disappear under proper lighting. The full-size frame (7.87ft × 13.12ft) supports wrap-around configurations for dead-angle-free shooting.

Setup is quick and straightforward: snap-on connection rods slot together without tools, and the whole thing breaks down into a compact carrying bag when you're done. The backdrop itself is machine washable, soft, foldable, and durable enough for regular use. Whether you're shooting YouTube content, running Zoom calls, doing product photography, or keying out backgrounds for film work — this kit covers it.
